=====================
Effectiveness Metrics
=====================

Evaluating the economic and physical performance of transactive systems requires multi-dimensional effectiveness metrics.

Key Effectiveness Metrics
-------------------------

1. **Peak Demand Reduction**:
   Quantifies the peak power reduction (kW) achieved through coordinated demand response during high-price or constrained hours.

2. **Energy Cost Savings**:
   Compares total energy procurement costs under transactive market coordination against static flat-rate tariffs.

3. **Renewable Utilization**:
   Measures the fraction of on-site solar PV generation consumed locally or stored in batteries rather than curtailed or backfed at low compensation rates.

4. **Thermal Comfort Compliance**:
   For building HVAC and water heating assets, measures the temperature deviation outside occupant deadband constraints over the simulation horizon.
